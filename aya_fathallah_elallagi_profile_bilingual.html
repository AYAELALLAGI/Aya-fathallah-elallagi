آية فتح الله العلاقي (Aya Fathallah Elallagi) طالبة هندسة كهربائية وإلكترونية ليبية في جامعة طرابلس. تشمل اهتماماتها الأكاديمية معالجة الإشارات، والذكاء الاصطناعي، والبحث الهندسي، والتقنية.

Aya Fathallah Elallagi is a Libyan Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the University of Tripoli. Her academic interests include signal processing, artificial intelligence, engineering research, and technology.

Her work includes research on signal reconstruction from undersampled data and projects that combine engineering and practical technology solutions. She has also participated in leadership and volunteering activities.

Education

Elallagi studies Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Tripoli, Libya. Her academic work includes engineering, digital signal processing, and computational methods.

Research

Her research interests include signal reconstruction, undersampling, machine learning, and applications of artificial intelligence to engineering problems.

One of her research projects investigates CNN-based signal reconstruction from undersampled data and compares learned reconstruction with Whittaker–Shannon interpolation. The work considers both band-limited signals and signals containing components above the reduced Nyquist frequency, where aliasing can occur.

Projects

دليل بلديتك (Your Municipality Guide) is a civic-technology project developed for Libyan municipalities. The project helps citizens identify where administrative documents can be obtained and what required documents or copies are needed. It focuses on guidance and information rather than performing government transactions electronically.

Leadership and volunteering

Elallagi has participated in scouting and Girl Guides activities and leadership-development initiatives. Her volunteering interests include youth development, leadership preparation, teamwork, and community service.
